Pasea - Password Generator

PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: September 8, 2026

1. OVERVIEW

This Privacy Policy applies to the Pasea mobile application, developed by The Gentleman Developer (hereinafter referred to as the "App"). The App functions strictly as an offline/local password generation tool designed to help users generate random passwords based on selected criteria (such as numbers, symbols, and letters) and copy them to their device's clipboard.

2. INFORMATION COLLECTION AND USE

We believe in total privacy. The App DOES NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, sensitive user data, or device information.

  • No Personal Data: We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or location data.
  • No Input/Output Tracking: All password generation occurs entirely locally on your device. The options you select (letters, numbers, symbols) and the passwords generated or copied are never stored, logged, or transmitted outside your device.
  • No Account Required: You can use all features of the App without creating an account or logging in.

3. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES AND PERMISSIONS

  • No Third-Party Analytics/Ads: The App does not integrate third-party analytics software, tracking tools, or advertising networks.
  • Permissions: The App requires no special or sensitive permissions to operate.

4. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Since our App does not collect any personal information, it is safe for users of all ages and fully complies with applicable child privacy regulations.

5. CHANGES TO THIS PRIVACY POLICY

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, the updated policy will be reflected here.
