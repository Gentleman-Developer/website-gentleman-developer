Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Allah Reminder ("the App") is developed and published by **Gentleman Developer**.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Allah Reminder handles information when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

Allah Reminder is designed to work without requiring you to create an account or provide personal information.

The App does not collect, store, or share personal information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Precise location
  • Contacts
  • Photos or media
  • Account credentials
  • Payment information

2. Data Stored on Your Device

Allah Reminder may store information such as your task progress and dhikr counter values locally on your device.

This information is used only to provide the App's functionality, such as:

  • Saving your tasks
  • Saving completed tasks
  • Saving your dhikr counter
  • Maintaining your progress within the App

This locally stored information is not transmitted to our servers and is not accessible to us.

3. Data Sharing

Allah Reminder does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

The App does not currently use user accounts, cloud synchronization, or third-party analytics services.

4. External Links

The App may provide a link to the developer's Instagram profile.

When you choose to open an external link, you may be redirected to a third-party website or application. Third-party services operate independently and have their own privacy policies and terms.

Allah Reminder does not control how third-party services collect or process information. We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any external service you use.

5. Data Security

Because the information used by the App is stored locally on your device, the security of that information also depends on the security of your device and Android operating system.

We do not transmit your locally stored task or counter data to our own servers.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

The information stored by Allah Reminder remains on your device until it is deleted through the App, removed by the user, or the App's data is cleared or the App is uninstalled.

Because the App does not maintain user accounts or store your data on our servers, we do not retain a separate copy of your local App data.

7. Children's Privacy

Allah Reminder does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Since the App does not require users to provide personal information, no personal information is knowingly collected through the App.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App or its data practices.

When changes are made, the updated policy will be published on this page.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of Allah Reminder, you can contact the developer at:

  • Developer: Gentleman Developer
  • Email: gentlemandeveloper@outlook.com
